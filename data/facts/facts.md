## H. Fee schedule (effective 1 March 2025 unless stated)

### H1. Commission by service and price slab

Commission is one flat percentage of the full property price, chosen by the slab in which the price falls.

| Slab | Price range | Buying (SVC-BUY) | Selling (SVC-SELL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slab 1 | Up to PKR 10,000,000 | 2.0% | 2.5% |
| Slab 2 | PKR 10,000,001 to PKR 25,000,000 | 1.75% | 2.25% |
| Slab 3 | Above PKR 25,000,000 | 1.5% | 2.0% |

Minimum commission: PKR 150,000 for buying; PKR 200,000 for selling. If the percentage amount is lower, the minimum applies.

### H2. Rental commission (SVC-RENT)

- Landlord pays 50% of one month's rent.
- Tenant pays 50% of one month's rent.
- Minimum PKR 10,000 for each side.

### H3. Other fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Admin fee (buying and selling) | PKR 7,500 per transaction |
| Documentation support (SVC-DOC) | PKR 35,000 per transaction |
| Property management fee (SVC-PM) | 8% of monthly rent collected |
| Property management minimum | PKR 6,000 per month |
| Investment advisory consultation (SVC-INV) | PKR 20,000 per 60-minute session |
| Written portfolio review (SVC-INV) | PKR 60,000 |

### H4. Valuation fee tiers (SVC-VAL)

| Property value | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to PKR 10,000,000 | PKR 15,000 |
| PKR 10,000,001 to PKR 25,000,000 | PKR 25,000 |
| Above PKR 25,000,000 | PKR 40,000 |

Commercial property valuations add PKR 10,000 to the tier fee.

### H5. Discounts

- Referral discount: 10% off the company commission for a client introduced by an existing client, up to PKR 75,000.
- Early-bird discount: 20% off the buying commission when the booking token is paid within 7 days of the first viewing.
- Referral and early-bird discounts cannot be combined; the larger discount applies.
- Discounts do not apply to valuation, documentation, advisory or admin fees.

Government charges are payable by the client and are not included in company fees. No rates are stated in company documents.

## I. Payment plans

Surcharge is a percentage of the property price, added to the total payable.

| Plan | Down payment | Instalments | Duration | Frequency | Surcharge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PP-A | 40% | 4 | 12 months | Quarterly | 0% |
| PP-B | 30% | 8 | 24 months | Quarterly | 4% |
| PP-C | 20% | 24 | 24 months | Monthly | 6% |
