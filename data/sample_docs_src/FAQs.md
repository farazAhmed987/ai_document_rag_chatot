## 4. Selling

**Q15. How does the company sell my property?**
A consultant prepares the listing, photographs and describes the property, shows it to interested buyers, attends the viewings and presents each offer to you. You decide whether to accept any offer. The work is done under a written listing agreement.

**Q16. How long does the listing agreement last, and can I end it early?**
The agreement lasts 90 days and can be renewed. If you wish to cancel it, send 7 days written notice.

**Q17. What does the company charge a seller?**
The selling commission is one flat percentage of the sale price: 2.5% up to PKR 10,000,000, 2.25% from PKR 10,000,001 to PKR 25,000,000, and 2.0% above PKR 25,000,000. The minimum is PKR 200,000, and an admin fee of PKR 7,500 applies per transaction.

**Q18. What would I pay if I sold a property for PKR 20,000,000?**
The price falls in the middle band, so the rate is 2.25%. The commission is PKR 20,000,000 x 2.25% = PKR 450,000. With the admin fee of PKR 7,500, the total is PKR 457,500.

**Q19. What if my property sells for a low price?**
The minimum protects the company on small sales. For a sale at PKR 6,000,000, the percentage would give PKR 150,000, which is under the minimum, so the commission is PKR 200,000.

**Q20. Should I get a valuation before I list?**
Many owners do, because a written value helps them set a realistic asking price. The valuation department reports separately from the sales teams, so its figure is not shaped by any commission. See the valuation questions below for the fees.

## 5. Renting and property management

**Q21. How does the company handle a rental?**
The consultant advertises the unit, shows it, checks that landlord and tenant understand the terms and prepares the tenancy paperwork. Rental questions go to rentals@himalayacrest.example or +92 51 5550 1005.

**Q22. Who pays the rental commission?**
Both sides share it equally. The landlord pays half of one month's rent and the tenant pays half of one month's rent. Each side pays at least PKR 10,000. On a rent of PKR 90,000, each side therefore pays PKR 45,000. On a rent of PKR 12,000, half would be PKR 6,000, so each side pays the PKR 10,000 minimum.

**Q23. How big is the security deposit, and when do I get it back?**
The deposit is 2 months of rent. It is returned within 14 days after the tenant has handed the unit back.

**Q24. How much notice must a tenant or landlord give?**
A tenant must give 30 days notice. A landlord who wants to end the tenancy must give 60 days notice.

**Q25. Who pays for repairs?**
The tenant covers minor repairs up to PKR 10,000 per item. Larger repairs are the responsibility of the owner. Owners who use the management service can leave the coordination of repairs to the management team.

**Q26. What does property management cost?**
The management fee is 8% of the monthly rent collected, with a minimum of PKR 6,000 per month. At a rent of PKR 150,000 the fee is PKR 12,000. At a rent of PKR 45,000, 8% would be PKR 3,600, which is below the minimum, so PKR 6,000 is charged. The two figures meet at a rent of PKR 75,000.

**Q27. How can an owner end the management arrangement?**
Either side may end it by giving 45 days notice. Please send the notice to rentals@himalayacrest.example.

## 6. Payments and payment plans

**Q28. How much is the booking token?**
The token is 2% of the property price. For HCR-RWP-0205 (price PKR 24,500,000) it is PKR 490,000. Since 1 January 2024 the rate has been 2%; an older rate that applied before that date is no longer in force.

**Q29. Is the token an extra cost?**
No. The token is part of the price you pay, and it is counted toward your down payment. On HCR-ISB-0108, for example, the down payment is PKR 8,400,000, of which the token of PKR 420,000 is already paid, leaving PKR 7,980,000 to pay.

It is a percentage of the property price, added to the total you pay. On a PKR 10,000,000 property, plan PP-C adds PKR 600,000, so the total becomes PKR 10,600,000. Plan PP-A adds nothing.

**Q32. What happens if I miss an instalment date?**
You have a grace period of 10 days after the due date. After that, a penalty of 0.1% of the overdue instalment is charged for each day, up to a ceiling of 5% of that instalment. On an overdue instalment of PKR 500,000, the penalty is PKR 500 per day and stops at PKR 25,000.

**Q33. Can the company cancel my booking for non-payment?**
Only after an instalment has been overdue for 60 days and the company has given 15 days written notice.

**Q34. Can I cancel a booking and get my money back?**
Yes, by written request emailed to bookings@himalayacrest.example. The refund depends on timing:
- Within 7 days of the booking date: the token is refunded less a 10% deduction, so 90% comes back.
- From day 8 to day 30: the token is refunded less a 50% deduction, so 50% comes back.
- After day 30: the token cannot be refunded.

**Q35. What is the refund on a token of PKR 295,000 cancelled on day 10?**
Day 10 falls in the second window, so 50% is deducted. The refund is PKR 147,500.

**Q36. What about money paid beyond the token?**
Amounts paid over and above the token are refunded less a 15% deduction. If you had paid PKR 2,000,000 beyond the token, you would receive PKR 1,700,000.

**Q37. How long does a refund take?**
The refund is paid within 21 working days of approval. Working days are Monday to Friday.

**Q38. Can I pass my booking to someone else?**
Yes, subject to a transfer fee of 1% of the property price, with a minimum of PKR 50,000. Transfers are not allowed in the first 30 days after booking, and processing takes 10 working days. For HCR-RWP-0205, the fee is 1% of PKR 24,500,000, which is PKR 245,000.

## 10. Local terms

**Q54. What is bayana?**
Bayana (also spelled bayanah or bayaana) is advance money paid to book a property. It is the same thing as the booking token, which is 2% of the price.

**Q55. What are iqrarnama and registry?**
An iqrarnama (iqrar nama, ikrarnama) is the written agreement between buyer and seller. The registry (registri) is the registered sale deed that records the transfer.

**Q56. What are intiqal and fard?**
Intiqal (intikal) is mutation, the update of the ownership record after a sale. A fard (fard-e-malkiyat) is an extract of the land record showing who the owner is.

**Q57. What do kiraya, kiraydar and malik mean?**
Kiraya (kiraaya) is rent, a kiraydar (kirayedar) is a tenant and a malik is an owner.

**Q58. What do qeemat, qist and rasid mean?**
Qeemat (keemat, qimat) is price, a qist (kist) is one instalment and a rasid (raseed) is a receipt.

**Q59. What are a naqsha and a mukhtarnama?**
A naqsha (nakshah) is a site plan or layout map. A mukhtarnama (mukhtar nama) is a power of attorney document.

**Q60. How big is a marla, a kanal, a lakh and a crore?**
One marla is 272.25 sq ft. One kanal is 20 marla, or 5,445 sq ft. A lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10,000,000. So a 7 marla plot measures 1,906 sq ft, and a price of PKR 21,000,000 is PKR 2.1 crore.

Please quote only the current values.
