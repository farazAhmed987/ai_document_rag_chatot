## 1. About Himalaya Crest Realty

Himalaya Crest Realty (Pvt.) Ltd., known to clients and colleagues as HCR, is a real estate company headquartered in Islamabad, Pakistan. It was founded on 14 March 2012 by Farhan Mirza, who began with a team of 6 people in a single rented office and a simple promise: every property deal should be explained in plain language, written down properly and handled without surprises.

Fourteen years later the company serves clients in four cities, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Abbottabad and Lahore, through four offices. Since 2012 it has sold 1,862 units and served 3,740 clients. It employs 86 staff members across sales, documentation, valuation, client services, property management, finance, compliance and advisory work, and it looks after 214 properties for owners under its management service.

HCR works across the full life of a property transaction. Buyers use the company to find and book homes, plots and commercial space. Sellers use it to list, market and negotiate. Landlords and tenants use it to find each other and, in the case of landlords, to hand over day-to-day management. Owners and investors use its valuation and advisory teams to understand what a property is worth and whether it suits their goals. The company has also launched six projects of its own, among them Pine Ridge Villas in Abbottabad, Crest View Residency in Islamabad, Saffron Heights in Rawalpindi and Lotus Gardens in Lahore.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The early years (2012 to 2015)

HCR opened its doors on 14 March 2012 in Islamabad. The first two years were spent building a client base through neighbourhood referrals, careful record keeping and a habit of attending every viewing in person. On 20 January 2013 the head office received its real estate agent licence, number HCR-REA-ISB-2013-0192, which allowed the company to expand its list of services with confidence.

On 1 September 2014 the company opened its Rawalpindi branch, a short distance from the capital but a distinct market with its own buyers, sellers and local customs. The branch quickly became the company's second centre of activity. On 9 June 2015 HCR joined the Fictional Association of Realty Professionals, known as FARP, under membership number FARP-2015-3381, committing itself to the association's published standards of conduct.

### 2.2 Growth in the north and the first project (2016 to 2018)

The Abbottabad branch opened on 12 May 2016. The hill city offered a different kind of property: larger plots, family villas and small commercial units serving a settled local population. On 18 October 2017 the company launched its first project of its own, Pine Ridge Villas, a development of villas and plots in Abbottabad. The project taught the team how to coordinate sales, documentation and construction updates under one roof, a lesson that shaped every later launch.

On 22 November 2018 the company reached 500 clients served. To mark the milestone, the leadership team reviewed every complaint received in the previous twelve months and used the findings to rewrite the client handbook.

### 2.3 New cities and new services (2019 to 2021)

The Lahore branch opened on 3 February 2019, extending the company's reach into the country's second largest city. Its licence number is HCR-REA-LHR-2019-0207. On 15 August 2020 the company established a dedicated valuation department, which operates under valuation practice licence HCR-VAL-2020-0035. The department produces written valuation reports for buyers, sellers and owners and has completed 2,410 valuations to date.

On 1 April 2021 HCR launched its property management service, taking on the collection of rent, the coordination of repairs and the reporting duties that busy owners often cannot manage themselves. The service started with a handful of owners and now covers 214 properties.

### 2.4 Larger projects (2022 to 2024)

On 11 July 2022 the company announced Crest View Residency in Islamabad, a residential development arranged in two blocks, Block A and Block B. It was the largest project the company had attempted. On 30 September 2023 HCR sold its 1,000th unit, a milestone reached after eleven and a half years in business.

On 5 March 2024 Saffron Heights was launched in Rawalpindi, bringing compact and family apartments to a growing part of the city. In the same year the company opened a Client Services Centre at its head office, a dedicated team that answers client questions about bookings, payment schedules and documents.

### 2.5 Advisory and handovers (2025 and 2026)

The investment advisory department was established on 2 January 2025. Its advisors meet clients in one-to-one sessions to discuss goals, timelines and the mix of property types that might suit them. On 20 August 2025 the company launched Lotus Gardens in Lahore, a project of apartments and a penthouse.

In 2026 the first handovers of completed units began. The company issued version 4.0 of its documents effective 1 June 2026, and several plots and a commercial unit are scheduled for handover before the end of the year.

## 3. Mission and values

### 3.1 Mission

The mission of Himalaya Crest Realty is to make property buying, selling and renting in northern and central Pakistan clear, documented and fair for every client. The company summarises this in its slogan: "Clear deals, firm ground."

### 3.2 Values

The company's five core values appear on the wall of every office and are reviewed with all staff at the start of each year.

1. **Transparency.** Fees, payment schedules and conditions are written down before a client commits. If a number changes, the client is told why.
2. **Accountability.** Every booking has a named consultant and every complaint has a named owner. Nobody is passed from desk to desk.
3. **Patience.** A property is usually the largest purchase in a family's life. Consultants are expected to give clients time to compare, to visit again and to ask the same question twice.
4. **Local knowledge.** Each branch is led by someone who knows its streets, its builders and its customs. Advice about a Lahore canal-side apartment is not given from Islamabad.
5. **Respect for the client.** Clients are treated as capable adults. Consultants explain options, state their own recommendation openly and leave the decision to the client.

### 3.3 How the values show up in practice

The values are not decoration. The documentation team will not forward a booking until the client has received the written payment schedule. The valuation team writes its reports without input from the sales team, so that a figure is never shaped by a commission. The complaints process, described in the Policies and Terms document, gives clients a named contact and fixed response times. The Client Services Centre keeps a record of every question it receives, and the monthly review of those questions often leads to changes in how forms and brochures are written.

### 5.2 How a typical transaction moves through the company

A buyer's first contact is usually with a sales consultant at a branch. The consultant arranges viewings, explains the property and shares the written payment schedule. When the client decides to proceed, the documentation team prepares the booking papers and the finance team records the booking token and issues a receipt. After booking, the Client Services Centre becomes the client's main point of contact for payment reminders and document questions. If the client later wishes to let the property, the property management team can take over its day-to-day care. At any stage the client may request an independent valuation from the valuation department, which reports separately from sales.

### 5.3 Decision-making

Major decisions, such as launching a project, changing a fee schedule or opening a new office, are taken at the monthly leadership meeting and recorded in writing. Fee and policy changes are published with an effective date and the previous values are kept in a revision history so that clients can see what changed and when.

## 11. Community programmes

### 11.1 Clear Deal Workshops

Since 2020 the company has held free workshops for first-time buyers and sellers. Each session lasts about two hours and covers how to read a payment schedule, what the usual stages of a transaction are and what questions to ask a consultant. Sessions are held at the head office and at each branch in turn, and attendance is open to the public without any obligation to buy or sell.

### 11.2 Student Site Visits

HCR invites groups of architecture and civil engineering students to visit its project sites twice a year. The visits allow students to see foundations, structural work and finishing stages first hand, guided by the company's project staff. The programme began with a group from Abbottabad in 2018 and has since been extended to all four cities.

### 11.3 Neighbourhood Clean-up Days

Once a year each branch organises a clean-up day in a neighbourhood where it has a project or an office. Staff and neighbours collect litter, repaint boundary walls and tidy shared green spaces. The Abbottabad branch has held its clean-up around the Pine Ridge site every year since 2018.

### 11.4 Clear Language Guides

The Client Services Centre publishes short printed guides that explain common property terms in plain language, including Roman Urdu equivalents such as bayana, iqrarnama, intiqal and fard. The guides are available free at every office and are updated whenever a policy changes.

Clients who need a quick answer about a viewing should note that viewings can be cancelled with at least 24 hours notice, which allows the consultant to release the time slot for another client.

The Client Services Centre in Islamabad can be reached on +92 51 5550 1002 and handles questions about booking receipts, payment reminders and document checklists. The centre opened in 2024 and is staffed during head office opening hours.

Seller listing agreements run for 90 days and can be renewed. The listing agreement can be cancelled by giving 7 days written notice.
