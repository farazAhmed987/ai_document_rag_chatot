## 1. How to read this document

This document lists the 24 properties currently carried in the Himalaya Crest Realty inventory across Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Abbottabad and Lahore. Section 2 gives a summary table of every property and Section 3 gives a second table with token amounts and handover dates. Section 4 describes each property in turn.

Every property has an ID in the pattern HCR-ISB-0101, where the three letters show the city (ISB for Islamabad, RWP for Rawalpindi, ATD for Abbottabad, LHR for Lahore). Sizes are given in marla and square feet; one marla is 272.25 sq ft and one kanal is 20 marla, or 5,445 sq ft. Prices are given in full digits, followed in the detailed sections by the same figure in crore, where one crore is 10,000,000.

The status of a property is one of three words. **Available** means the property can be booked. **Reserved** means a client has booked it and it cannot be booked by someone else unless the reservation is cancelled. **Sold** means the sale is complete; sold properties are kept in this document for reference only.

Commission, admin fees and other charges are explained in the Services and Fees document. Booking, refund and transfer rules are explained in the Policies and Terms document. Government charges are payable by the client and are not included in company fees.
